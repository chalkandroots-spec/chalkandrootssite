02 · THE SAID INVESTIGATION

s + ai + d → said

In said, ai represents /ĕ/, as in bed.

💬 Said means spoke or told. “I said hello to my friend.”

Say the sounds: /s/ /ĕ/ /d/. Tap one finger for each sound. There are three sounds; the two letters ai represent the middle sound.

03 · EXPLAIN YOUR EVIDENCE
