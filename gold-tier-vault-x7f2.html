AI-Assisted Frameworks

AI does not automatically create learning. Use these teacher prompts to draft support around the text set, then check every draft against the passages and your students' needs.

  1. Model reasoning: “Using only this passage, draft a claim–evidence–reasoning model. Quote the evidence exactly and explain how it supports the claim.”
  2. Scaffold: “Create three levels of sentence starters for a claim, evidence, reasoning, and counterclaim. Keep the thinking with the student; do not supply their finished response.”
  3. Plan feedback: “Draft two feedback questions for a student whose evidence is relevant but whose reasoning is unclear. Ask questions that help them revise.”

Check quotations, accuracy, reading level, and fit before sharing. Remove student names and identifying details before using an AI tool. AI drafts. You teach.

Test-Taking Strategies

  1. Read the whole question; notice words such as BEST or NOT.
  2. Return to the passage and identify evidence before choosing.
  3. For select-two questions, check that you selected exactly two answers.
  4. For two-part questions, make sure the evidence supports your Part A answer.
  5. For hot text, read the surrounding sentences before selecting one.
  6. Plan your claim, evidence, reasoning, and counterclaim before writing.
  7. Skip a difficult question and return if the testing rules allow it.
  8. Review answers and written work when time remains.
