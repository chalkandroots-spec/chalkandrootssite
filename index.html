About Angella

Meet Angella Barngrover

Educator • Science of Reading • AI observer • Founder of Chalk & Roots. My work blends decades of teaching experience, ELD support, curriculum design, technology, storytelling, and practical teacher resources. I have lived through the major eras of educational technology, beginning with FORTRAN in Jamaica, and experiencing the rise of personal computers, the internet, and now AI.

"Technology will continue to evolve, but one thing remains constant: great teaching changes lives."
