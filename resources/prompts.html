How to Use This Kit

  1. Pick the prompt below that matches what you need today: a full lesson, a hook, ELD support, a standards check, or an exit ticket.
  2. Copy the prompt into your AI tool of choice (ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini all work).
  3. Fill in the bracketed [placeholders] with your grade, subject, and skill.
  4. Read the output and adjust it for your students. AI drafts. You teach.
  5. Save your favorite versions so next time takes even less than 5 minutes.
2

The 5 Prompts

Prompt 1 Full Lesson

The Core Lesson Plan Builder

Build a [length]-minute [grade] [subject] lesson on [skill]. Include a Science of Reading–aligned mini-lesson, a small group activity for ELD students, and an exit ticket.

Use this when you need a full lesson from scratch, fast.

Prompt 2 Opening Hook

The Garden-Based Hook

Write a 3-minute garden-based or hands-on hook to open a lesson on [skill] for [grade] students. Tie it to something they can touch, grow, or observe.

Use this when your opening needs more energy than a warm-up slide.

Prompt 3 ELD Support

The ELD Small Group Support

Design a 10-minute small group activity that supports ELD students practicing [skill] in [grade] [subject]. Include sentence frames and one visual support.

Use this when your ELD students need more scaffolding than the whole-group lesson gives them.

Prompt 4 Standards Check

The Standards Alignment Check

Check this lesson plan against the [state/Common Core] standard for [skill] in [grade]. List what's missing and suggest one fix for each gap.

Use this when you already have a lesson and just need a second set of eyes on alignment.

Prompt 5 Exit Ticket

The Exit Ticket Generator

Write a 3-question exit ticket that checks understanding of [skill] for [grade] students. Include one recall question, one application question, and one short-answer question.

Use this when class is ending in 5 minutes and you still need a quick check.
