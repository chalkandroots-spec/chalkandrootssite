Story Builder

Build your story one sticky note at a time. Fill in each box before you start drafting — Character, Setting, Problem, Events, Ending.

1

Character

Who is your story about? Give one detail that makes them feel real — a want, a fear, a habit, a flaw.

2

Setting

Where and when does your story happen? Paint the picture in one or two sentences.

3

Problem

What goes wrong? This is the thing your character has to solve or face.

4

Events

What happens next? List 3–4 key moments in order, one sticky note per moment.

Ending

How does your character solve the problem (or how does the story resolve)? Wrap it up in one or two sentences.

✨ Bonus — Show, Don't Tell

Pick one moment from your Events section and rewrite it using an action, a piece of dialogue, or a physical detail instead of a "telling" sentence like "she was scared."
